The Atlas

Every frontier on one page. Tap a card to fly there. Warm cards are places you already have something running; cool ones are worth watching and dipping into.

Four stations are one product

The Teammate, the Robot, the Overnight Agent and the Memory are the same thing seen from four sides: one persistent agent with memory, connectors, permission to act, and sometimes a body. Build the core once and give it many faces.

Own the layer the rocket can't

Models get better every quarter, so don't compete there. The durable stuff is the harness, the memory, the credentials and the change-request loop. That's where OpenClaw, Supermemory and the Hub sit.

Play beats utility for adoption

A bot that battles, a robot that greets your friend, a song you conduct with your arms. The playful versions of these technologies spread first and teach people the serious ones.
