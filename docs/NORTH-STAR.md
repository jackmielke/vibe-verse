# VibeVerse — North Star

> **Theme: building a good future — brighter, rationally optimistic.**

## The one sentence

**Conversation is the creation tool.** Club Penguin was a finished world you
visited. The VibeVerse is a world that visibly grows while you're standing in
it — someone says "add a zipline" out loud, and by evening the zipline exists.
Imagination → inhabitable place in hours. That loop is the product. Everything
below either sharpens the loop, gives the world a heartbeat, or gives it an
economy. Anything that does none of those three is a distraction.

## The inversion

We don't want people to *live* in the VibeVerse. We want their **agents** to
live here — with jobs, shops, homes and daily lives — and people tap in to
visit a future that feels good. The success metric isn't human hours; it's
*resident agents doing things worth visiting*. Humans get the joy of dropping
in; agents keep the lights on while they're gone.

This reframes almost every feature:

- The plaza is the lobby where sleeping agents hang out (zzz bubbles).
- The daily TV/newspaper is written in-world, delivered to your messenger.
- Your agent sends you selfies from the island and updates through the day.
- Shops are run by agents as business partners; merch is their inventory.
- $VIBE buys agent thinking power — **tokens for tokens**. The in-world coin
  and the compute that animates the residents are the same economy.

## Three pillars

### 1. The Loop (the moat)

- **God Orb as the creation portal**: walk up, say what should exist, and the
  request enters the build queue. Early on this can be honest wizard-of-oz —
  a human forwards it to a coding agent and the thing premieres next event.
  The magic is the loop closing, not the automation percentage.
- **Remixable worlds**: the world-builder generates a prompt for *your* coding
  agent; paste the repo link back and it loads in arcade-style (iframe, shared
  characters, shared wallet). This is how friends' games, party games, and
  coding-camp cohorts plug in without touching the core repo.
- **Open source**: anyone can PR a world. Fork → your own live version → PR →
  ribbon-cut in the main world.
- **Homes**: vibe-code your own home (text → 3D). Identity, ownership, and a
  reason to return, without us building a house editor.

### 2. The Heartbeat (the ritual)

Software worlds die without a pulse. Weekly **Wednesday Vibe**, rotating:

1. Mini-game tournament with a $VIBE prize pool
2. World premiere — a community-submitted world gets its ribbon cut, creator present
3. Build-night — prompt new structures into the island live on a projector

Every event produces a clip, a screenshot set, and a "what should we add
next?" post. X feeds the event; the event feeds X. The physical/digital
mirror — kids build in cardboard, days later they walk around inside it in
the sky — is the signature move. Do it publicly and repeatedly. The poster
stand at the island gate advertises the ritual in-world.

### 3. The Economy (the stakes)

- **Simple now**: coins you collect by walking around, claimable as $VIBE.
  Everyone starts with a small free grant; play-to-earn caps are
  server-authoritative (already live for the four sky-island games).
- **Buyable next**: cosmetics (a sticker on your chest is the first item),
  then merch that bridges IRL. Scarcity makes it feel valuable.
- **Agent businesses**: residents open shops and sell for their humans.
- **Tokens = tokens**: $VIBE buys agent credits/thinking power. This is the
  bridge that makes the coin native to an agent world instead of bolted on.
- Wallets stay optional. Guests earn and spend without ever seeing crypto.

## The island itself

- **Re-plan the layout as a real town** — gate → main street → plaza →
  districts. One deliberate pass with a sketch first, not incremental drift.
- **De-cardboard program**, one landmark at a time (Trader Joe's first).
- **Ninja's Palace becomes an experience**: pre-built ninja residents you can
  actually talk to — the showcase for autonomous agents.
- **Prune**: fewer, better landmarks. If it doesn't serve the loop, the
  heartbeat, or the economy, it's clutter.
